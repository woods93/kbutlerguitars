Cheetah Chrome Signature ’64

Punk rock OG legend Cheetah Chrome of The Dead Boys is seen here with his signature CC ’64. The Dead Boys storied history began in the ’70s when punk rock was new, raw and rebellious. The title of their first record, “Young, Loud and Snotty,” could not have been more true to its name. The Dead Boys are killing it in front of sold-out venues all over the world again, and this guitar is keeping it loud and snotty night after night.
